Qnow: AI-Powered Patient Triage & Queue Optimization System
Qnow is an intelligent, real-time patient queue management system designed to optimize hospital workflows and ensure that critical patients receive immediate care. By leveraging an AI Triage Filter, the system automatically analyzes incoming patient data to determine condition severity, calculate queue priority, and intelligently route them to the appropriate medical department.

🌟 Key Features
~AI-Driven Triage: Replaces traditional first-come-first-serve models by automatically assessing patient symptoms and prioritizing life-threatening or critical cases.
~Real-Time Queue Management: Powered by WebSockets to provide live, instant updates on patient status, changing wait times, and active department queues.
~Smart Department Routing: Instantly recommends the correct medical department based on the AI's analysis of the patient's condition.
Comprehensive Staff Dashboard: An intuitive, multi-tab interface featuring dedicated portals for the main Dashboard, Doctors, Live Queue, and Patient Registration.

💻 Tech Stack
Frontend: React (Vite), TypeScript, React Router, Socket.IO Client.
Backend: Python, Flask, Flask-SocketIO.

Qnow is built to modernize hospital waiting rooms with a dynamic, severity-based approach, drastically reducing wait times for emergency cases and improving overall healthcare operational efficiency.
